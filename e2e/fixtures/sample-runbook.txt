Runbook: Zephyr Queue Backlog Alert

Service: zephyr-notification-worker
Owning team: Messaging Platform

When the ZephyrQueueBacklog alert fires, the on-call engineer should first run
`zephyrctl queue status --queue=notifications` to confirm the backlog size.

The documented emergency mitigation for this specific alert is to scale the
zephyr-notification-worker deployment to 12 replicas using:

    kubectl scale deployment/zephyr-notification-worker --replicas=12

Do not scale above 12 replicas without checking with the Messaging Platform team
first, since the downstream email provider rate-limits at 12 concurrent workers.

If the backlog does not drain within 15 minutes of scaling, escalate to the
Messaging Platform on-call via the #messaging-platform-oncall channel.
